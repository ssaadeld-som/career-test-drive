      Where the numbers come from

      Wages: BLS Occupational Employment & Wage Statistics, May 2024 (national and state). Growth and openings: BLS Employment Projections 2025–35. Tasks, work context, skills: O*NET 31.0 (CC BY 4.0). Majors, schools and graduates' earnings: U.S. Dept. of Education College Scorecard; majors linked to jobs with the NCES CIP–SOC crosswalk. AI agents write the explanations, and a checker rejects any number that isn't in this data.

      Good to know

      This is a starting point, not career advice. Talk to people in the job, a school counselor, or a family member before you decide. Licensing rules vary by state. Schools are ranked by graduates' earnings, not prestige.
