The problem

Teenagers choose majors based on myths

"Lawyers argue in court every day and make $300k right out of school."

"Game designers play games all day."

"Electricians don't make much money."

Confident, specific, and wrong. Nobody checks them before the student commits years and tuition.

Safety

Built for kids, tested with attacks

  • Invented numbers: the validator catches them and the model retries; the student never sees one (caught "$300,000" live)
  • Prompt injection: a free regex layer, then the Gatekeeper
  • Distress: points to a trusted adult and the 988 Lifeline
  • Personal info, off-topic: politely blocked
  • Loops and overspend: per-agent caps; $0.05 per session, $2 per day
  • 8 of 8 live and offline scenarios stopped · 17 automated tests pass
